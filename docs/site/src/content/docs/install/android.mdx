<Aside type="note">
	The Android app is in development and is not documented yet. Its debug builds appear on the
	[releases page](https://github.com/Mark7888/two-place-paste/releases), but they are not ready
	for everyday use.
</Aside>

It uses the same groups, relay and encryption as the desktop app, so a phone will join your
existing group like any other device.

## Try the demo

You can look around the Android app without a relay. On a fresh install, the bottom of the first
screen has a **Try a demo** link. It starts a demo group that lives only inside the app: a
simulated relay and two simulated devices, **Demo laptop** and **Demo tablet**. Nothing leaves the
phone, and the app makes no network requests while the demo runs.

Every tab works in the demo:

- **History** and **Store** start with a few sample entries and items.
- About every 90 seconds while the app is open, a simulated device copies a new line of text.
- **Devices** lists the group, and you can revoke a simulated device, which re-keys the group.
- **Pairing** → **Show a pairing code** is answered by a simulated device a few seconds later.
  Reading a code from another device needs a real device and a real relay.
- The quick-settings tile syncs with the demo group.

A banner above every tab says you are in the demo. To leave it, open **Settings** and tap
**Close demo**: the demo's group, history and store are discarded and the app returns to its
first screen. The demo is also discarded whenever the app is closed.

The demo uses keys of its own. Your phone's real keys are not touched, so you can close the demo
and then join your real group as usual.

The link is only offered until the phone first joins a real group. After that it does not
come back, even if you leave the group, unless you clear the app's storage or reinstall it.
