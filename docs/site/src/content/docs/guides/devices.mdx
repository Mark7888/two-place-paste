The **Devices** tab lists every device that holds the group's key, with the current key
generation (*epoch*).

<Image src={devices} alt="The Devices tab listing Work laptop and Home desktop" class="screenshot" />

## Revoke a device

Do this when a computer is lost, sold, or simply no longer yours to sync with.

<Steps>

1. On any other device in the group, open **Devices** and press **Revoke** next to the device.

2. Read the dialog. It names the device you are removing and lists the devices that keep access.

   <Image src={revoke} alt="The dialog confirming the revocation of Home desktop" class="screenshot" />

3. Press **Revoke …**.

</Steps>

What happens:

- A **new group key** is generated and given to the remaining devices. The epoch goes up by one.
- The revoked device **loses access immediately**. It cannot read anything written from now on and
  cannot rejoin without being paired again.
- Entries written **before** the revocation stay readable to whoever already had them, and expire
  within 24 hours.
- Your clipboard is not touched.

<Aside>
	**Leave the group** in Settings is different: it only deletes the keys on *this* device. The
	group still lists it, and its old key would still work. To shut a device out for good, revoke it
	from another device.
</Aside>
