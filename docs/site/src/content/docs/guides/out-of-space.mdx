Your group's room on the relay is set by whoever runs it. The desktop app tells you when it is
running low, and how to get more.

## Operator website

If the relay's operator has a website, the bottom of the sidebar has **Open operator website**,
with the site's address underneath. Every notice on this page offers the same button. It opens the
site in your browser with your group's id added to the address (`?group_id=…`), so the operator
can tell which group you are in — for example, to give that group more room.

The link belongs to the relay of the group you are using. After you switch to
[another group](/two-place-paste/guides/groups/), it is the new group's relay's, or gone if that
relay names no website.

## Banners

Two banners at the top of the app are advice: nothing has failed yet.

- **Your store is almost full** — the group's [store](/two-place-paste/guides/store/) holds 90% or
  more of its size or item limit. Delete items you no longer need, or ask your relay operator for
  more room.
- **History is full** — the relay removed older [history](/two-place-paste/guides/history/)
  entries before their 24 hours were up, to make room for new ones. It says how many.

<Image src={banner} alt="The Store tab with a banner saying the store is almost full, an Open operator website button, and the link at the bottom of the sidebar" class="screenshot" />

**×** closes a banner until the next time it applies: the next entry removed early, or the next
save or delete that leaves the store still that full. To stop them altogether, turn on **Hide
out-of-space banners** in [Settings](/two-place-paste/guides/settings/#behaviour).

## When a save or upload is refused

A refusal is shown in a window, whatever the banner setting says, until you close it:

| Title | What happened |
|---|---|
| **The store is full** | The item did not fit in the group's store. Delete some items, or ask your relay operator for more room. |
| **The relay's storage is full** | The group's store had room, but the relay's storage for every group's store together did not. Only the operator can fix this. |
| **The relay can't store this right now** | The relay could not take a clipboard upload. History makes room by removing old entries, so this is the relay itself — its disk, or other uploads still in progress — and usually passes. Try again later; if it keeps happening for a few minutes, contact your relay operator. |

<Image src={popup} alt="A window titled The store is full, with Open operator website and OK buttons" class="screenshot" />

The window also appears for an automatic upload that was refused while the app was closed, the
next time you open it.
