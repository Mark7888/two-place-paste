A device syncs with one group at a time. If you share a clipboard with two circles — work and
home, say — the desktop app can keep both groups and let you switch between them, so you don't
have to leave one and pair again to reach the other.

Only one group is **in use** at a time. The others are **set aside**: their keys stay on this
computer, but nothing syncs with them, and to their other devices this computer looks offline.

## Turn it on

Open **Settings** and turn on **Keep several groups**, under Behaviour. A **Groups** tab appears in
the sidebar.

<Image src={groups} alt="The Groups tab: Office in use, Home and Family set aside, each with Switch and Remove" class="screenshot" />

## Add a group

<Steps>

1. In **Groups**, press **Add a group…** and then **Continue to Pairing**.

   The group in use is set aside with its keys.

2. In **Pairing**, [create a group](/two-place-paste/guides/create-a-group/) with a link from your
   server admin, or [pair with a device](/two-place-paste/guides/add-a-device/) that is in the
   group you want.

</Steps>

This computer joins the new group as a new device, with its own keys. The groups it is in can't
tell that they share a computer.

## Switch groups

In **Groups**, press **Switch** next to a group that is set aside. The app disconnects from the
group in use, sets it aside, and connects to the other one as it would on a fresh start. History,
Store and Devices then show the new group.

A group that changed while it was set aside catches up when you switch back: new entries are
there, and if a device was revoked in the meantime, this computer picks up the new key.

## Remove a group

Press **Remove…** next to a group that is set aside. Its keys are deleted from this computer. To
use that group again, pair with a device that is still in it.

The group in use is left from **Settings → Leave the group**, as before. With several groups,
leaving only affects the group in use; the ones set aside keep their keys.

<Aside type="caution">
	Removing a group is local only, like leaving one. The relay still lists this computer in that
	group. To shut it out for good, [revoke it](/two-place-paste/guides/devices/) from another device.
</Aside>

## A group you can no longer use

If this computer was revoked from the group in use, or the server admin deleted the group, the
Groups tab marks it as **can't be used**. Switching or adding a group then deletes its keys instead
of setting it aside, and the app asks first.

## Turn it off

Turning **Keep several groups** off hides the Groups tab and nothing else. The group in use stays in
use, and the others keep their keys on this computer: turn the setting back on to see them again.

An older version of the app that doesn't have this setting opens the group that was last in use.
