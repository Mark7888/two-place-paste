The **Sync** tab shows what is on this computer's clipboard, the group's newest entry, and three
buttons. The same actions are in the tray menu.

<Image src={sync} alt="The Sync tab with Sync now, Upload and Download buttons" class="screenshot" />

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Sync now** | Compares when this clipboard last changed with the age of the group's newest entry, and goes whichever way is newer. The line above the buttons says which way that will be. |
| **Upload** | Encrypts this clipboard and sends it to the group. It becomes the newest entry. |
| **Download** | Puts the group's newest entry on this clipboard. |

## When Sync now has to ask

Windows and macOS do not record *when* something was copied. If the app has not seen the clipboard
change since it started, it cannot know which side is newer, so **Sync now** asks:

<Image src={ask} alt="The dialog asking whether to Sync up or Sync down" class="screenshot" />

From the tray, the same question arrives as a notification with **Upload this clipboard** and
**Download the latest** buttons. Those two items also appear in the tray menu for a few minutes, in
case notifications are off.

## Syncing automatically

Two switches in [Settings](/two-place-paste/guides/settings/) make syncing hands-free. Both are off
by default:

- **Watch the clipboard** — anything you copy is uploaded straight away.
- **Take what other devices upload** — a new entry from another device replaces this clipboard as
  soon as it arrives.

Turn on both on every device for a fully shared clipboard, or only *watch* on one and only *take*
on another for one-way sync. Content the app put on the clipboard itself is never uploaded back.

Automatic syncing has a size limit (25 MB each way by default). A bigger entry is left for you to
send with **Upload** or fetch with **Download** or [History](/two-place-paste/guides/history/); a
notice at the top of the app says what was held back.

## Pausing auto-sync

**Pause auto-sync for 5 min** on the Sync tab, or **Pause auto-sync for 5 minutes** in the tray
menu, holds both automatic directions at once:

- nothing you copy is uploaded on its own — not during the pause and not after it;
- a new entry from another device is not put on your clipboard; a notice says one arrived, and
  **Download** or [History](/two-place-paste/guides/history/) gets it.

**Upload**, **Download** and **Sync now** still work. While paused, a notice above every tab says
until when, the tray icon shows a pause badge, and the same button and menu item read **Resume
auto-sync**. The pause ends on its own after five minutes, and when the app restarts.

<Image src={paused} alt="The Sync tab while auto-sync is paused, with a notice and a Resume auto-sync button" class="screenshot" />

Pause before copying something you do not want on your other devices, such as a password from a
browser extension.

## Passwords and other sensitive copies

A copy from a password manager, or one that matches a
[sensitive-content filter](/two-place-paste/guides/sensitive-content/), is never uploaded
automatically. The Sync tab shows it masked, with an eye button to reveal it, and **Upload** asks
"push anyway?" before sending it.

## What can be synced

Text, images and files, up to 50 MB per entry. Anything over 10 MB is sent in pieces and shows a
progress bar.

<Aside>
	Entries expire 24 hours after they were written. TwoPlacePaste is for moving things between your
	devices, not for keeping them.
</Aside>
