title: Getting started
description: From nothing to your first shared paste in about ten minutes.
---

import { Steps, Aside, LinkCard, CardGrid } from '@astrojs/starlight/components';

TwoPlacePaste has two parts: a **relay server** that you (or someone you trust) runs, and the
**desktop app** on each of your computers. Your devices form a **group** that shares one
encryption key. Whatever one device uploads, the others can paste.

<Aside type="tip" title="Already have a relay?">
	If someone already runs a relay for you and gave you a link, skip step 1.
</Aside>

<Steps>

1. **Run a relay server.**
   You need a machine with Docker and a domain name. The
   [Docker guide](/two-place-paste/self-hosting/docker/) takes about five minutes, plus however long
   your reverse proxy takes to get a certificate.

2. **Install the desktop app** on your first computer.

   <CardGrid>
   	<LinkCard title="Windows" href="/two-place-paste/install/windows/" />
   	<LinkCard title="macOS" href="/two-place-paste/install/macos/" />
   </CardGrid>

3. **Create a group.**
   Sign in to the relay's admin page, generate a creation link, and paste it into the app's
   **Pairing** tab. → [Create a group](/two-place-paste/guides/create-a-group/)

4. **Add your other devices.**
   Install the app on the second computer, then pair it with a code shown by the first one.
   → [Add a device](/two-place-paste/guides/add-a-device/)

5. **Copy, sync, paste.**
   Copy something on one computer and press **Sync now** on the **Sync** tab (or in the tray
   menu). Press it on the other computer and paste.
   → [Syncing the clipboard](/two-place-paste/guides/sync/)

</Steps>

Want it hands-free? Turn on **Watch the clipboard** on one device and **Take what other devices
upload** on the other in [Settings](/two-place-paste/guides/settings/).
